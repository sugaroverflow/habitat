# Clover 29 — Design Inspiration Brief

## Core reference
Use the former San Francisco home as the personality baseline:
- warm, cozy, playful soft-maximalism
- lots of plants, books, art, personal objects, and layered display
- creamy upholstery and pale wood
- oversized Persian/oriental-style rugs
- blush, sage/mint, powder blue, lavender
- office/bedroom adds vaporwave pink/cyan ambient lighting, neon, graphic art, and tech/gaming elements

## Evolved London direction
Preserve the personality but do not recreate the previous flat exactly.

### Color
Use muted but still saturated colors rather than candy-bright pastels or generic beige:
- dusty sage
- jade / teal
- faded blush
- dusty rose
- warm cream
- butter / marigold
- muted terracotta / coral
- soft blue

The palette should feel dustier, softer, and lower-contrast than the previous home, but still colorful.

### Pattern and cultural influence
Bring in Oriental / South Asian / Indo-Persian influence through:
- Persian-style rugs
- botanical and floral motifs
- Mughal-inspired painted details
- arches
- lattice or carved textures
- decorative borders
- heirloom-feeling pattern and ornament

## Overall target
Soft-maximalist personality + quieter saturation + Indo-Persian romanticism.

Keep vaporwave ambient lighting mainly for the office/bedroom rather than making the whole flat neon.

## Avoid
- generic beige minimalism
- overly candy-colored pastel interiors
- duplicating the previous apartment room-for-room
- sterile or overly polished styling
